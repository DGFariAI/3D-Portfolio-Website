There is a way of working that looks like faith from the outside and functions like exhaustion on the inside.

You have probably met it. Perhaps you have lived it. It goes something like this: you take the vision, you take the talent, you take the burden, and you carry all three yourself, and you call the carrying obedience.

It is not obedience. It is orphan work. And it will run you into the ground with a clear conscience, which is the most dangerous kind of ruin there is.

## For Him, or with Him

Building **for** God and building **with** God look almost identical on a Tuesday. Same desk. Same hours. Same spreadsheet.

The difference is in who is holding the outcome.

When you build for Him, every result is a report card. The launch is a verdict. The quiet quarter is an accusation. You are perpetually auditioning for someone who has already adopted you, and no amount of output ever quite settles the question, because output was never what settled it.

When you build with Him, the work becomes a place of company rather than a place of proof. You still work hard. You may work harder. But you are not carrying the weight of being the guarantor of your own life.

> He did not call you into partnership so that you could do it alone with His logo on it.

## The Counsellor is not a last resort

Here is something I had to learn slowly, and I would rather you learned it quickly.

Most of us treat the Holy Spirit like roadside assistance. We drive alone, we make our own turns, and then when the engine gives out at midnight we call for help and wonder why the journey was so lonely.

But He was offered as Counsellor. As Advocate. As the One who guides into truth. That is not emergency language. That is board meeting language. That is a standing invitation to consult before the decision rather than after the wreck.

So consult. Actually consult. Not as a formality you perform over a decision you already made, but genuinely, with the willingness to hear no.

## What this looks like on an ordinary Tuesday

Practical, or it is just sentiment:

1. **Decide before you decide.** Bring the choice while it is still a choice. Prayer offered after the contract is signed is not counsel, it is a press release.
2. **Let the pace be set from outside your anxiety.** Anxiety is a terrible project manager. It always says now, and it is almost always lying.
3. **Do the next right thing, not the whole thing.** You were given a step. You were not given the staircase. That is on purpose, and it is mercy, because you could not carry the staircase.
4. **Keep a record.** Write down what you believed you were told and what actually happened. Faith grows on evidence, and you will forget the evidence by spring.

## The freedom in it

Here is what I want you to take away.

If the work is yours alone, then its failure is your identity. That is a crushing way to build, and it explains why so many capable people quit right before the turn.

But if the work is His and you are in it with Him, then failure is tuition, delay is preparation, and the outcome rests with Someone who has never once been surprised.

That does not make you passive. It makes you dangerous. Because a person who is not terrified of the result will take the risks that the result actually requires.

Build with Him. Not merely for Him. The work will be better, and so will you.
