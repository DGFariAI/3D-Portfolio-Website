Here is something worth thinking about.

Most people do not choose their life. They accept it. There is a world of difference between those two words, and the distance between them is where your whole future lives.

Drifting is not a decision you make once. It is a decision you avoid making, over and over, until the avoiding becomes the answer. Nobody wakes up and says, "today I will begin wasting the next ten years." That is not how it works. It happens by degrees. A small compromise here. A postponed conversation there. A dream you stop mentioning because it started to embarrass you.

And then one day you look up and you are somewhere you never chose.

## The current is always running

You do not have to do anything to drift. That is the trouble with it. The current is always running, and it is always running away from the thing you were made for.

Every person alive is being carried somewhere. The only question is whether you picked the direction.

> You will not accidentally become who you were made to be. Nobody arrives at purpose by neglect.

I want you to sit with that, because it cuts against almost everything the culture tells you. We are told to relax and let it unfold. To trust the process. But a process you never chose is not a process. It is just weather.

## What God calls, God equips

Now here is where I part ways with the ordinary version of this advice.

The world will tell you to find yourself. I want to suggest something better: you were already found. You were known before you were formed. The question was never "who do I want to be." The question is "who was I made to be, and have I said yes to it yet."

That reframing changes the weight of the thing. Because if your purpose is something you invent, then it rests entirely on you, and no wonder you are tired. But if your purpose is something you were assigned, then the One who assigned it has a stake in your finishing it.

You are not building alone. You never were.

## Three questions worth answering this week

Do not answer these quickly. Quick answers are usually borrowed from somebody else.

1. **What am I doing right now that I would not choose again?** Not what is hard. Hard is fine. What is merely inherited.
2. **What have I been calling a season that is actually a decision?** We are very good at describing our choices as circumstances.
3. **If nothing changed for five more years, would that be alright?** Your gut answers this one before your mind does. Listen to the gut.

## Begin badly

The last thing, and it matters.

You will want to wait until you are ready. You will not be ready. Readiness is something you acquire on the road, not something you pack before you leave.

Start before the plan is complete. Start while your hands still shake. The person you are becoming is built out of the small, unglamorous, badly executed first attempts of the person you are now.

Do not wish it were easier. Wish you were better. And then go be better, starting today, in one small way you can actually name before you go to bed tonight.

That is not drift. That is direction. And direction, held long enough, becomes a destination.
